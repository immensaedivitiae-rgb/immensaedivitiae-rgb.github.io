Privacy Policy: Aura Board

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Developer: Thriveyboys

The short version. Aura Board has no accounts and asks for no name, email or phone number. Your recordings never leave your phone. The only thing we receive is problem reports, so we can fix bugs. Ads are shown by Google AdMob.

This policy explains what information the mobile app "Aura Board" (the "app") collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer, Thriveyboys.

1. Information we collect

  • A random ID. The app creates an anonymous ID so problem reports from the same phone can be grouped together. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Problem reports. If the app hits an error, or you send a report from inside the app, we receive the error message or your written message, the app version, your phone model and operating system and a few recent error messages. Please don't put personal information in a report.

Microphone and recordings

The app uses your microphone only when you tap record, so you can make your own sounds. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. If you share a sound, your phone's share menu and the app you pick handle it; we don't see what you send.

Information collected by our partners

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking in Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first; if you say no, you still get ads, but not personalized ones.
  • Purchases (Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat). Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat receives a purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID so it can confirm what you bought and restore it.
  • App updates (Expo). When the app checks for updates, Expo's update service receives basic technical information such as your IP address, app version and platform.

Payments

  • We never collect or store your payment information. Purchases in the app (such as Remove Ads) are paid through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, using the payment method saved in your Google or Apple account. Your card number, bank details, PayPal login and billing address go only to Google or Apple. They are never sent to us or to our game server.
  • Ways to pay without typing in a card. Depending on your country, Google Play and the App Store let you pay with PayPal, gift cards (Google Play or Apple gift cards bought in stores), your phone carrier bill, or other methods they offer. You choose and manage these in your Google or Apple account, not in our app.
  • What we receive. Only a purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID (through RevenueCat), so the app can unlock what you bought and restore it on a new phone. The receipt does not contain your name, card or bank details.
  • Refunds are handled by Google or Apple under their refund policies.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your settings, favourites and recordings.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't send push notifications from a server.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we never upload your recordings.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To find and fix bugs, using problem reports.
  • To show ads and process purchases through the partners listed below.

4. How long we keep information

Problem reports are kept until the problem is fixed, then deleted or unlinked from your ID.

5. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • In the app: use "Delete my data" in the app's settings. This erases your sounds, recordings and progress on this phone and unlinks every problem report from you. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored.
  • Without the app: email immensaedivitiae+aura@gmail.com. We'll handle it within 30 days.
  • Recordings: delete them in the app, or uninstall the app to remove them all.
  • Ads: on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and pop-up ads.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Brazil), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to how it's used. Email us to use these rights.

6. Children

The app is for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated for teens. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and our database is locked so the app can only add reports, never read anyone's data.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
