Privacy Policy: Nah, You Saw That?

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Developer: Thriveyboys

The short version. You can play without signing in. Other players only ever see the username you pick. If you sign in with Google or Apple to back up your progress, we receive your email address, and from Google usually your name and profile photo too. We use these only to sign you in and keep your backup, never show them to other players and never use them for marketing. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. You can delete your data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what information the mobile game "Nah, You Saw That?" (the "app") collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer, Thriveyboys.

1. Information we collect

Created automatically when you first open the app

  • A random player ID. The app creates an anonymous ID so the game server can tell players apart. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

Information you choose to give us

  • Username. If you use the friends features, you pick a username (letters, numbers and _ only). We ask you not to use your real name. Usernames are checked for offensive words.
  • Friend code, friends list, friend requests and blocks. These are stored so the friends leaderboard works. Blocking is private: a blocked player is not told.
  • Game results. For each daily puzzle set: your score, time, number of hints used and your streak. Friends you have added can see these on the leaderboard.
  • Problem reports. If the app hits an error, or you send a report from Profile → Report a problem, we receive the error message or your written message, the app version, your phone model and operating system, the screen you were on and a few recent error messages. Please don't put personal information in a report.
  • Sign-in and cloud backup (optional). Signing in is done only through "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple". There is no separate email and password sign-up. When you sign in, we receive and store:
    • your email address (with Apple you can choose "Hide My Email", and we then receive a private relay address from Apple instead);
    • your Google or Apple account ID;
    • from Google, usually your name and profile photo; from Apple, your name only if you choose to share it;
    • the date and time you signed up and last signed in.
    We then back up your game progress (coins, gems, streak, paintings, badges and settings) so you can restore it on a new phone. We use your sign-in details only to sign you in and keep your backup. They are never shown to other players, never sold and never used for marketing or emails.

Information collected by our partners

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking in Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first; if you say no, you still get ads, but not personalized ones.
  • Purchases (Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat). Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat receives a purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID so it can confirm what you bought (for example, Remove Ads) and restore it.
  • App updates (Expo). When the app checks for updates, Expo's update service receives basic technical information such as your IP address, app version and platform.

Payments

  • We never collect or store your payment information. Purchases in the app (such as Remove Ads and coin packs) are paid through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, using the payment method saved in your Google or Apple account. Your card number, bank details, PayPal login and billing address go only to Google or Apple. They are never sent to us or to our game server.
  • Ways to pay without typing in a card. Depending on your country, Google Play and the App Store let you pay with PayPal, gift cards (Google Play or Apple gift cards bought in stores), your phone carrier bill, or other methods they offer. You choose and manage these in your Google or Apple account, not in our app.
  • What we receive. Only a purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID (through RevenueCat), so the app can unlock what you bought and restore it on a new phone. The receipt does not contain your name, card or bank details.
  • Refunds are handled by Google or Apple under their refund policies.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your settings, puzzle progress and gallery are saved on your device.
  • The daily reminder is scheduled on your device. We don't send push notifications from a server.
  • When you share a result, your phone's share menu or the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or Instagram) handles it. We don't see what you send or who you send it to.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your phone number, contacts, the photos on your phone, precise location, microphone or camera.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game: daily puzzles, streaks, friends leaderboard, sign-in and cloud backup.
  • To keep the game safe and fair: rate limits, username filtering and blocking.
  • To find and fix bugs, using problem reports.
  • To show ads and process purchases through the partners listed below.

4. What other players can see

Only your username, and for friends you have added: your daily score, time, hints and streak. Players who add you by username must send a request, which you can accept, decline or block. Your email, name, profile photo, sign-in account and social media accounts are never shown in the app.

5. How long we keep information

  • Your username, friends and results: until you delete your data, or until you stop using the app and we remove inactive accounts.
  • Rate-limit records (used to stop spam): deleted automatically after 2 days.
  • Problem reports: kept until the problem is fixed, then deleted or unlinked from you.
  • Cloud backup and sign-in details (email, account ID, name, photo): until you delete your data.
  • Our server provider keeps short-term security logs (such as IP address and sign-in time) to protect against abuse.

6. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete my data. This permanently removes your username, friends, friend requests, blocks, results, cloud backup and sign-in details (email, account ID, name and photo) from our server, and signs you out. Past problem reports are unlinked from you. Your progress on your phone stays unless you uninstall the app.
  • Without the app: email immensaedivitiae+nyst@gmail.com with your username or friend code and ask us to delete your data. We'll do it within 30 days.
  • Ads: on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and pop-up ads.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Profile, or in your phone's notification settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Brazil), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to how it's used. Email us to use these rights.

7. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated for teens. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database is locked so the app can only use specific checked actions, and players can't read each other's data directly. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For important changes we'll also let you know in the app.
